## Source and language review limits

- Every admitted teaching link includes the complete current card snapshot, a literal claim excerpt in each language and the actual fact/card IDs. The three safety-source-only items have empty source_cards, so a broad topic match cannot claim the card taught them.
- Current-bank faults were not copied: the negative ears question, the bones-cannot-feel explanation, all-heavy-stones-have-the-same-weight explanation, untranslated ice options, and the freezer negative question were replaced. Existing source-bank files remain unchanged.
- The plant need items avoid asserting that all plants need fertilizer or soil. The goat item avoids generalizing daily drinking behavior to all animals. No source-card link was invented for adult help with glass or hot cookware.
- EN/TL/BIS card bodies were read for every included source. Additional corpus searches checked mapaso, gunting, giuhaw, sudlanan, pislit, gisi, pilo, gapas and matunaw. Corpus malagom had no hits and was not used. Attestation is evidence of usage, not native approval; newly composed sentences still need native review.
- Text questions contain their own conditions or recall familiar objects; none requires an absent picture. No child is instructed to test heat or handle broken glass. Read-aloud must include all options.
- Families shared with existing Grade 3-material items keep their existing identifiers: animal-drinking-water, pressing-soft-clay, rubber-band-stretching and broken-glass-sharp-edges. The different broken-glass source facts must not make one concept eligible twice in a form.
- Source-checked means model author review of narrow content and provenance. Native, teacher, cohort and student-pilot gates remain pending. No mastery cutoff or causal app-effect claim follows.

Primary safety references checked online:

- [U.S. Fire Administration: Fire Safety for Children](https://www.usfa.fema.gov/prevention/home-fires/at-risk-audiences/children/) — hot objects and keeping children away from burn hazards.
- [Utah State Fire Marshal: Match and Lighter Safety](https://firemarshal.utah.gov/services/cigarette-fire-safety/match-and-lighter-safety/) — the school-age safety section includes reporting broken glass to a trusted adult rather than picking it up.
