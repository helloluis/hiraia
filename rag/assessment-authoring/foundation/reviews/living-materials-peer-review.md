## P2: ha-f3-0018 — wind protection becomes generic roof shelter in Tagalog

The English question concerns protection from strong wind. Its Tagalog key is `Ilipat sa lugar na may silong`; the explanation similarly uses `lugar na may silong`. A roofed or shaded place can remain exposed to strong wind. The source card, `dcard-12115 / protecting-plants-g3`, specifically supports protection from strong wind and rain in all three languages. Make wind protection explicit rather than relying on a roof/shelter word to supply it.

Proposed keyed option:

- EN: `Move it to a place protected from strong wind`
- TL: `Ilipat sa lugar na protektado sa malakas na hangin`
- BIS: `Ibalhin sa dapit nga adunay panalipod batok sa kusog nga hangin`

Proposed explanation:

- EN: `A place protected from strong wind helps protect the plant.`
- TL: `Nakatutulong sa pagprotekta sa halaman ang lugar na protektado sa malakas na hangin.`
- BIS: `Makatabang sa pagpanalipod sa tanom ang dapit nga adunay panalipod batok sa kusog nga hangin.`

This preserves the narrow knowledge and existing safety limit. It does not add a direction for a child to move a heavy pot or enter dangerous weather.

## P2: ha-f3-0022 — the stem asks what matches, including unobserved properties

The current statement gives a red cup and a red plate, then asks `What matches?` The intended answer is color, but it does not establish that their weights differ; depending on the objects, shape descriptions may also overlap. The claim limit disclaims an unmentioned weight comparison, but that limit is not visible to the child. Ask what the stated color word describes. The source `dcard-09052 / classifying-solids-by-color-g3` directly supports red as a color.

Proposed stem, retaining Color/Shape/Weight options and the current explanation:

- EN: `A cup and a plate are both red. What does “red” tell us about them?`
- TL: `Parehong pula ang tasa at plato. Ano ang inilalarawan ng “pula”?`
- BIS: `Parehong pula ang tasa ug plato. Unsay gihulagway sa pulong nga “pula”?`

The question then has one supported answer without requiring an unprovided image or mass measurement.
