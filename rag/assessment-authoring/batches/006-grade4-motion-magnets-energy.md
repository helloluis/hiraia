### English

In 4 seconds, car A travels 8 meters and car B travels 4 meters. Which has the greater average speed?

- A: Car A
- B: Car B
- C: They are equal.

**Explanation:** Both traveled for the same time. Car A covered more distance, so its average speed was greater.

### Tagalog

Sa 4 na segundo, 8 metro ang nalakbay ng kotse A at 4 metro ng kotse B. Alin ang may mas mataas na average speed?

### Cebuano

- B: Awto B

**Explanation:** Pareho ang gidugayon sa pagbiyahe. Mas layo ang nabaktas sa awto A, mao nga mas taas ang average speed niini.
