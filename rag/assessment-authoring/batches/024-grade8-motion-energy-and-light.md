How is average acceleration found?

### English

In air, what does a glass convex lens do to parallel light rays near its central axis?

- A: Brings them together toward a focus
- B: Spreads them farther apart
- C: Leaves all their directions unchanged

**Explanation:** A glass convex lens in air converges nearby parallel rays toward its focal region. This does not mean every object seen through it is magnified.
