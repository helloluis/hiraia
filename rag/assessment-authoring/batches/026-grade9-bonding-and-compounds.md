### English

What distinguishes a chemical change from a change of shape alone?

- A: A new substance forms
- B: The same substance is cut smaller
- C: The same substance is bent

**Explanation:** A chemical change produces a new substance. Cutting or bending alone changes shape without that chemical change.

### Tagalog

Ano ang kaibahan ng chemical change sa pagbabago lamang ng hugis?

- A: May nabubuong bagong sangkap
- B: Pinuputol nang mas maliit ang parehong sangkap
- C: Binabaluktot ang parehong sangkap

**Explanation:** May bagong sangkap na nabubuo sa chemical change. Ang pagputol o pagbaluktot lamang ay pagbabago ng hugis.

### Cebuano

Unsay kalainan sa chemical change ug sa pag-usab lamang sa porma?

- A: Adunay maporma nga bag-ong substansiya
- B: Giputol og mas gagmay ang samang substansiya
- C: Gibaliko ang samang substansiya

**Explanation:** Ang chemical change makahimo og bag-ong substansiya. Ang pagputol o pagbaliko lamang mag-usab sa porma.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Changes size while retaining the substance.
- C: Changes shape while retaining the substance.

**Selection decision:** Remove unqualified completely different and repair the bank’s smaller mistranslations; do not use irreversibility as the definition.

### English

Two solutions react and a new solid forms in the liquid. What is that solid called?

- A: Solvent
- B: Precipitate
- C: Gas bubble

**Explanation:** A precipitate is a solid that separates from a solution. In this example, the reaction forms a solid that does not stay dissolved.

### Tagalog

Nagreact ang dalawang solution at may bagong solid na nabuo sa likido. Ano ang tawag sa solid na iyon?

- A: Solvent
- B: Precipitate
- C: Bula ng gas

**Explanation:** Ang precipitate ay solid na humihiwalay mula sa solution. Sa halimbawang ito, bumubuo ang reaksyon ng solid na hindi nananatiling dissolved.

### Cebuano

Nagre-react ang duha ka solution ug adunay bag-ong solid nga naporma sa likido. Unsay tawag nianang solid?

- A: Solvent
- B: Precipitate
- C: Bula sa gas

**Explanation:** Ang precipitate usa ka solid nga mobulag gikan sa solution. Niining pananglitan, ang reaksyon makahimo og solid nga dili magpabiling dissolved.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Names the dissolving medium rather than the new solid.
- C: Names a gas rather than a solid product.

**Selection decision:** Entirely new scored question: shipping-bank search found a data-table prompt and a soap/distilled-water negative prompt, neither tests this definition. State the reaction rather than infer it from every visible solid. No mixing instructions are given.

**Language evidence:** Author self-review using existing source vocabulary; native-language and teacher reviews remain pending.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

### English

Why can a solution of table salt in water conduct electricity?

- A: Its ions cannot change position
- B: Only whole neutral salt molecules carry charge
- C: Its dissolved ions can move and carry charge

**Explanation:** Dissolved sodium and chloride ions can move through the water and carry charge. This is different from electron conduction in a metal.
