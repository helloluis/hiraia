What does the esophagus do with swallowed food?

- A: Carries it to the stomach
- B: Chews it into pieces
- C: Absorbs most of its nutrients

**Explanation:** The esophagus is a muscular tube that carries swallowed food toward the stomach.

### Tagalog

- A: Dinadala ito sa tiyan
- B: Nginuya ito nang pira-piraso

**Explanation:** Ang esophagus usa ka tubo nga adunay kaunuran nga nagdala sa gilamoy nga pagkaon paingon sa tiyan.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Assigns the mouth/teeth chewing function to the esophagus.
- C: Assigns the small intestine’s main nutrient-absorption function to the esophagus.

**Selection decision:** Replace the legacy Cebuano swallowed-to-thrown mistranslation using the card’s attested gilamoy. No claim that gravity alone moves food.

**Language evidence:** Primary current card and bank question inspected in English, Tagalog and Cebuano. New wording preserves the narrow science claim; teacher and native-language review remain pending.

### English

How do hairs and mucus inside the nose help when you breathe?

- A: They make oxygen from dust.
- B: They send dust into the blood.
- C: They trap some incoming dust.

### Tagalog

Paano nakatutulong ang buhok at uhog sa loob ng ilong kapag humihinga?

- A: Gumagawa sila ng oxygen mula sa alikabok.
- B: Dinadala nila ang alikabok sa dugo.
- C: Nahuhuli nila ang ilang pumapasok na alikabok.

### Cebuano

- A: Mohimo sila og oxygen gikan sa abog.
- B: Ipadala nila ang abog ngadto sa dugo.
- C: Madakpan nila ang pipila sa mosulod nga abog.

**Explanation:** Ang buhok ug uhog makatabang sa pagdakop sa abog sa dili pa kini makaabot sa mas lawom nga agianan sa hangin. Dili nila makuha ang matag partikulo.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Confuses filtering particles with producing oxygen.
- B: Reverses the protective trapping function and treats dust as a substance intentionally absorbed into blood.

**Selection decision:** Remove the source’s humidifying-air distractor because humidification is also a real nasal function. Do not attribute warming solely to hairs or promise complete filtration.

**Language evidence:** Primary current card and bank question inspected in English, Tagalog and Cebuano. New wording preserves the narrow science claim; teacher and native-language review remain pending.
