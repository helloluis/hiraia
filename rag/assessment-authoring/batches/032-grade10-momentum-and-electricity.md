### English

In the ideal near-Earth model, a ball moves under uniform downward gravity with air resistance ignored. What happens to its horizontal velocity?

- A: It steadily decreases
- B: It stays constant
- C: It steadily increases

**Explanation:** Gravity acts downward. In this model, there is no horizontal net force to change the horizontal velocity.

### Tagalog

Sa ideal na modelong malapit sa ibabaw ng Earth, pareho at pababa ang gravity at hindi isinasama ang air resistance. Ano ang nangyayari sa horizontal velocity ng bola?

- A: Patuloy itong bumababa
- B: Hindi ito nagbabago
- C: Patuloy itong tumataas

**Explanation:** Pababa ang gravity. Sa modelong ito, walang horizontal net force na magbabago sa horizontal velocity.

### Cebuano

- A: Padayon kini nga mokunhod
- B: Dili kini magbag-o
- C: Padayon kini nga modako

**Explanation:** Paubos ang gravity. Niining modelo, walay horizontal net force nga magbag-o sa horizontal velocity.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Incorrectly gives gravity a horizontal slowing effect.
- C: Incorrectly gives gravity a horizontal speeding effect.

**Selection decision:** Keep the ideal-model condition. The source bank translates ignoring air resistance as no air; use the actual modeling assumption.
