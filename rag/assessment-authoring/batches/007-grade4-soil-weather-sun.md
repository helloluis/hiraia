### English

Which soil particles are the largest: sand, silt, or clay?

- A: Sand
- B: Silt
- C: Clay

**Explanation:** Sand particles are larger than silt and clay particles. Clay particles are the smallest of these three.

### Tagalog

- A: Sand
- B: Silt
- C: Clay

**Explanation:** Mas malalaki ang butil ng sand kaysa sa silt at clay. Pinakamaliit sa tatlo ang butil ng clay.

- A: Sand
- B: Silt
- C: Clay

**Explanation:** Mas dagko ang mga lugas sa sand kaysa sa silt ug clay. Ang mga lugas sa clay ang pinakagamay niining tulo.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Silt is finer than sand, although coarser than clay.
- C: Clay has the finest particles of the three, reversing the size comparison.

**Selection decision:** Revise the coarse-sand bank question into an explicit three-way particle-size comparison. Link recent exposure to the current card that actually teaches that comparison, rather than to a card merely naming coarse sand.

### Tagalog

- A: Nananatili lamang ito sa ibabaw.
- B: Hindi ito makapasok sa lupa.

### Tagalog

- A: Agad silang tumataas.
- B: Agad silang nagiging makahoy.
- C: Nalalanta at lumulupaypay sila.

- C: Malaya ug mokulpo sila.

**Explanation:** Ang tubig makatabang nga magpabiling lig-on ang humok nga mga bahin sa tanom. Mahimo silang malaya kon kulang sa tubig.

- A: Water shortage does not cause an immediate burst of stem and leaf growth.
- B: Lignification is not the immediate physical response of soft tissues losing water.

**Selection decision:** Limit to a possible response in a soft-stemmed plant. Do not assert that every wilted plant recovers merely by adding water or that all wilting has this cause.
