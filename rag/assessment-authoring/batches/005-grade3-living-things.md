### English

A kitten is taller and larger than it was a month ago. What does this show?

- A: Growth
- B: Reproduction
- C: Feeding

**Explanation:** An increase in a young animal’s size is a sign of growth. Having offspring is reproduction.

### Tagalog

Mas matangkad at mas malaki ang kuting kaysa noong nakaraang buwan. Ano ang ipinapakita nito?

- A: Paglaki
- B: Pagpaparami
- C: Pagkain

### Cebuano

- B: Pagsanay
- C: Pagkaon

**Explanation:** Timailhan sa pagtubo ang pagdako sa batan-ong hayop. Ang pagbaton og anak mao ang pagsanay.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Confuses the same kitten becoming larger with producing another kitten.
- C: Feeding supports growth, but the observation describes a change in body size rather than eating.

**Selection decision:** Use an observable size change rather than the source quiz’s unsupported right-after-birth sequence. Shares a growth-evidence family with the new-leaves item.

**Language evidence:** The primary source card and question were inspected in all three languages. Newly composed wording remains draft pending teacher and native-language review.
