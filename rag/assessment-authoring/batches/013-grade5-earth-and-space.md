Layers of sand are pressed together and cemented into rock. Which rock group does this form?

- A: Igneous rock

### Tagalog

- A: Igneous rock

- B: Sedimentary rock
- C: Metamorphic rock

**Explanation:** Usa kini ka paagi sa pagporma sa sedimentary rock. Ang latak magkadikit hangtod mahimong bato; dili eksaktong ingon niini maporma ang tanang sedimentary rock.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Requires molten-rock cooling rather than the stated sediment accumulation.
- C: Confuses compaction/cementation of loose sediment with metamorphic change of an existing rock.

**Selection decision:** Specify a sediment-based formation route rather than defining all sedimentary rock by visible layers. Cemented means natural mineral binding, not necessarily construction cement.

### English

Which process breaks rock down where it is?

- A: Weathering
- B: Erosion
- C: Deposition

**Explanation:** Weathering breaks down rock in place. Erosion carries material away, while deposition leaves transported material in a new place.

### Tagalog

Aling proseso ang dumudurog o sumisira sa bato sa kinaroroonan nito?

- A: Weathering
- B: Erosion
- C: Deposition

**Explanation:** Sinisira o dinudurog ng weathering ang bato sa kinaroroonan nito. Dinadala palayo ng erosion ang materyal, at iniiwan naman ito ng deposition sa bagong lugar.

### Cebuano

Unsang proseso ang magbuak o magguba sa bato sa dapit diin kini anaa?

- A: Weathering
- B: Erosion
- C: Deposition

**Explanation:** Ang weathering magbuak o magguba sa bato sa dapit niini. Ang erosion modala sa materyal palayo, samtang ang deposition mobiya sa gidala nga materyal sa bag-ong dapit.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Confuses local breakdown with transport of material.
- C: Confuses breakdown with settling/depositing transported material.

**Selection decision:** Do not define weathering only as a slow process or only as smaller physical pieces; chemical weathering also changes minerals. The added in-place condition disambiguates the school-level process names.

**Language evidence:** Current primary card and source bank question inspected in all three languages. New wording preserves the scored conditions and uses corpus-attested language or retained science terms; native review remains pending.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

**External check:** [National Park Service — Weathering and Erosion](https://home.nps.gov/articles/000/weathering-erosion.htm) — Weathering breaks down material; erosion moves the resulting particles. Questions isolate one process while acknowledging real processes may occur together.
