### English

What is the net force when all forces on an object balance?

- A: The largest individual force
- B: Zero
- C: The sum of force sizes, ignoring direction

**Explanation:** The force vectors add to zero. An object can be at rest or move at constant velocity when its net force is zero.

### Tagalog

Ano ang net force kapag balanse ang lahat ng puwersa sa isang bagay?

- A: Pinakamalaking indibidwal na puwersa
- B: Zero
- C: Kabuuan ng laki ng mga puwersa, hindi kasama ang direksiyon

**Explanation:** Zero ang kabuuan ng mga force vector. Maaaring nakahinto o gumagalaw sa constant velocity ang bagay kapag zero ang net force.

### Cebuano

Unsa ang net force kon balanse ang tanang puwersa sa usa ka butang?

- A: Kinadak-ang indibidwal nga puwersa
- B: Zero
- C: Kinatibuk-an sa kadako sa mga puwersa, wala tagda ang direksiyon

**Explanation:** Zero ang kinatibuk-an sa mga force vector. Mahimong nagpahulay o naglihok sa constant velocity ang butang kon zero ang net force.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Ignores cancellation among forces.
- C: Adds scalar magnitudes instead of combining force vectors.

**Selection decision:** Use the net-force definition; balance does not necessarily mean no motion.

**Language evidence:** Three-language author draft check; teacher and native-language review pending.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

**External check:** [OpenStax Physics — Newton’s First Law](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/4-2-newtons-first-law-of-motion-inertia) — Zero net force preserves velocity in an inertial reference frame; force is not required to sustain constant motion. ### English

A thermoelectric generator has a hot side and a cooler side. What useful output can it produce directly?

- A: Electricity
- B: A flow of newly made fuel
- C: A spinning mechanical shaft

**Explanation:** A thermoelectric generator converts some heat flowing across a temperature difference into electricity. Its generating element has no moving parts.

- C: Umiikot na mechanical shaft

**Explanation:** Ginagawang kuryente ng thermoelectric generator ang bahagi ng init na dumadaloy dahil sa magkaibang temperatura. Walang gumagalaw na parte ang generating element nito.
