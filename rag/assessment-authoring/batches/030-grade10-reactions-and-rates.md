**Family:** `physical-change-no-new-substance`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

**Correct option:** A (`o1`).

### English

Juice powder mixes with water without forming new substances. Which description is correct?

- A: A physical change: the substances keep their chemical identities
- B: A chemical change: a different color always means a new substance
- C: A chemical change: unseen powder must have become a new substance

**Explanation:** The powder spreads through the water while its substances keep their identities. That is a physical change. Different appearance does not by itself prove that new substances formed.

### Tagalog

Nahahalo ang juice powder sa tubig nang walang nabubuong bagong substance. Aling paglalarawan ang tama?

- A: Physical change: nananatili ang chemical identity ng mga substance
- B: Chemical change: laging bagong substance ang ibig sabihin ng ibang kulay
- C: Chemical change: tiyak na bagong substance ang powder na hindi na makita

**Explanation:** Kumakalat ang powder sa tubig habang nananatili ang identity ng mga substance nito. Physical change iyon. Hindi sapat na patunay ng bagong substance ang pagbabago ng itsura lamang.

### Cebuano

Ang juice powder masagol sa tubig nga walay bag-ong substance nga maporma. Unsang paghulagway ang husto?

- A: Physical change: magpabilin ang chemical identity sa mga substance
- B: Chemical change: ang lahi nga kolor kanunay nagpasabot og bag-ong substance
- C: Chemical change: ang powder nga dili na makita siguradong nahimong bag-ong substance

**Explanation:** Ang powder mokatag sa tubig samtang magpabilin ang identity sa mga substance niini. Physical change kana. Ang pagbag-o sa hitsura lamang dili igo nga pamatuod nga adunay bag-ong substance.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Mistakes a changed appearance or mixture color for conclusive evidence of chemical change.
- C: Mistakes loss of visible particles during dissolving for formation of new chemical substances.

**Selection decision:** Replace the prior unsupported color-change nuance target and its source snapshot, rather than preserve false exposure provenance. Revise an existing physical-dissolution classification item to the separately taught juice-powder example. Keep source_question_fact_id pointing to the actual bank candidate. This does not establish numerical equivalence or an interchangeable form with the old item.

**Language evidence:** Replacement authored by the peer-review agent after inspecting all three primary-source and selected-bank versions. This replacement itself has not received an independent review, native-speaker review or teacher approval. Parent reviewed replacement source, content and provenance during integration.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

**External check:** [OpenStax Chemistry 2e — Physical and Chemical Properties](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-3-physical-and-chemical-properties) — Physical change preserves chemical identities; appearance and even color can change without a chemical reaction.
