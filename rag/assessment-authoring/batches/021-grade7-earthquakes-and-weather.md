### English

What is a fracture along which blocks of rock have moved called?

### Tagalog

**Distractor review:**

- B: A fold is bending of rock layers, not this fracture with displacement.
- C: A volcanic vent is an eruption opening, not this fault definition.

**Selection decision:** Use the slip-bearing teaching source and add displacement to distinguish a fault from a generic crack. Remove crack as an overlapping source distractor.
