### English

Which system uses satellite signals to track positions on moving tectonic plates?

- A: GPS
- B: Seismometer
- C: Barometer

**Explanation:** Repeated GPS measurements show how positions change over time. Scientists use these changes to study plate motion.

### Tagalog

Aling system ang gumagamit ng satellite signals upang subaybayan ang posisyon sa gumagalaw na tectonic plates?

- A: GPS
- B: Seismometer
- C: Barometer

**Explanation:** Ipinapakita ng paulit-ulit na GPS measurements kung paano nagbabago ang posisyon sa paglipas ng panahon. Ginagamit ito upang pag-aralan ang plate motion.

### Cebuano

Unsang system ang naggamit og satellite signals aron masubay ang posisyon sa naglihok nga tectonic plates?

- A: GPS
- B: Seismometer
- C: Barometer

**Explanation:** Ang balik-balik nga GPS measurements nagpakita kon giunsa pagbag-o sa posisyon sa paglabay sa panahon. Gigamit kini aron tun-an ang plate motion.

**Distractor review:**

- B: A seismometer measures ground shaking rather than satellite-based position.
- C: A barometer measures atmospheric pressure rather than plate position.

**Selection decision:** Replace the negative bank stem and overlapping satellite-signals/radio-signals options. Do not claim measurements are perfectly exact or that one measurement establishes velocity.

### English

In mantle convection, warmer rock is less dense than the rock around it. Which way does it tend to move?

- A: Downward
- B: It stays in the same place
- C: Upward

**Explanation:** Less dense mantle material tends to rise relative to its surroundings. This movement is very slow and does not mean the whole mantle is liquid.

### Tagalog

Sa mantle convection, mas mababa ang density ng mas mainit na bato kaysa sa paligid nito. Saan ito karaniwang gumagalaw?

- A: Pababa
- B: Nananatili sa parehong lugar
- C: Pataas

### Cebuano

- A: Paubos
- B: Magpabilin sa samang dapit
- C: Pataas

**Explanation:** Kasagarang mosaka ang mantle material nga mas ubos ang density kaysa sa palibot. Hinay kaayo kini; wala kini magpasabot nga liquid ang tibuok mantle.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Reverses the buoyant direction for the stated relative density.
- B: Ignores the stated buoyancy in this convection model.

**Selection decision:** Use relative density, not total weight; keep the condition that surrounding material is denser. Avoid a boiling-liquid model of the entire mantle.
