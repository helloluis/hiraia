## ha-g4-0001 — Identifies herbivore as the diet category for a plant-eating animal.

**Revision / origin / status:** 1 / revise / source_checked.

**Target:** `g4-herbivore-meaning` · `g4-living_things-animal-diets` · internal `G4-L-6` (not an official DepEd identifier).

**Source:** `herbivore-plant-eater-g3`; cards: `ffct-09721`. Bank item version: 2.

**Question provenance:** `herbivore-plant-eater-g3` in the newer merged bank. A different card fact ID is an explicit reviewed knowledge link, not an ID substitution.

**Supporting evidence:** The source introduces herbivore through grass, leaves and fruit; the stem states the diet to be classified.

**Family:** `herbivore-diet-classification`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

**Use and eligibility:** Incoming grade 5 readiness using grade 4 material candidate; recent-learning candidate only after exposure to this knowledge. Supporting recall/recognition, not evidence of completing a practical activity.

**Correct option:** A (`o1`).

### English

What do we call an animal whose food is only plants?

- A: Herbivore
- B: Carnivore
- C: Omnivore

**Explanation:** Herbivore is the term for a plant-eating animal. Grass, leaves and fruit are plant foods.

### Tagalog

Ano ang tawag sa hayop na puro halaman ang pagkain?

- A: Herbivore
- B: Carnivore
- C: Omnivore

**Explanation:** Herbivore ang tawag sa hayop na kumakain ng halaman. Pagkaing halaman ang damo, dahon at prutas.

### Cebuano

Unsa ang tawag sa hayop nga puro tanom ang pagkaon?

- A: Herbivore
- B: Carnivore
- C: Omnivore

**Explanation:** Herbivore ang tawag sa hayop nga mokaon og tanom. Ang sagbot, dahon ug prutas mga pagkaon gikan sa tanom.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Carnivore is the animal-food category, not the plant-only diet stated.
- C: Omnivore includes both plant and animal foods; the stated diet includes only plants.

**Selection decision:** Retain the familiar classification but avoid asserting in the explanation that a real herbivore can never eat animal material.

**Language evidence:** Source-card and bank wording in all three languages was inspected. New wording is a draft; corpus evidence is not native-speaker approval.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

Institutional teaching resource; does not establish that individual animals never depart from a usual diet.
