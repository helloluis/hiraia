### English

A child gives a good shirt to a younger sibling who wears it. Which practice is this?

- A: Reusing
- B: Composting
- C: Recycling into new fabric

**Explanation:** The existing shirt stays useful as clothing. Giving it to another wearer is reuse.

### Tagalog

Ibinigay ng bata ang maayos na damit sa nakababatang kapatid na nagsuot nito. Anong gawain ito?

- A: Pag-reuse
- B: Composting
- C: Pag-recycle bilang bagong tela

**Explanation:** Nananatiling kapaki-pakinabang bilang kasuotan ang damit. Pag-reuse ang pagpasa nito sa ibang magsusuot.

### Cebuano

Gihatag sa bata ang maayong sinina sa manghod nga nagsul-ob niini. Unsang buhat kini?

- A: Pag-reuse
- B: Composting
- C: Pag-recycle ngadto sa bag-ong panapton

**Explanation:** Ang daang sinina nagpabiling mapuslanon. Pag-reuse ang paghatag niini sa laing magsul-ob.

**Distractor review:**

- B: No decomposition into compost occurs when another child wears the shirt.
- C: The shirt is not processed into newly manufactured fabric.

**Selection decision:** Add a same-purpose transfer example to the existing jar and scrap-paper family. The linked bank item asks about rags, so this is an additional form, not an unchanged reuse. Language self-review uses the primary-card term sinina consistently. This is not a finding that sapot is incorrect; corpus hits mainly concerned spider webs and did not establish this intended clothing use.

**Language evidence:** Primary source and relevant bank question read in English, Tagalog and Cebuano. Shared terminology with earlier batch forms; new sentences remain pending native-language review.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

A clean bag from a shop now carries a child’s notebooks. Which practice is this?

**Explanation:** Bag gihapon kini nga adunay laing gamit. Wala kini iproseso aron mahimong bag-ong papel.

**Distractor review:**

- A: No biological decomposition is described.

**Family:** `sound-from-vibration`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

### English

A drum keeps sounding briefly after it is struck. What is its stretched skin doing?

- A: Staying completely still
- B: Turning all the way around
- C: Vibrating back and forth

**Explanation:** The stretched skin vibrates after the strike. These vibrations help produce the drum’s sound.

### Tagalog

Sandaling patuloy na tumutunog ang tambol matapos paluin. Ano ang ginagawa ng nakaunat na balat nito?

- A: Nananatiling hindi gumagalaw
- B: Umiikot nang buong paikot
- C: Nagva-vibrate nang pabalik-balik

**Explanation:** Nagva-vibrate ang nakaunat na balat matapos paluin. Tumutulong ang vibration na ito sa paggawa ng tunog ng tambol.

### Cebuano

Nagpadayon kadiyot ang tingog sa tambol human kini bunalan. Unsay gibuhat sa inat nga panit niini?

- A: Nagpabiling wala molihok
- B: Nagtuyok sa usa ka tibuok tuyok
- C: Nagkurog nga balik-balik

**Explanation:** Ang inat nga panit magkurog human bunalan. Kini nga vibration makatabang sa paghimo sa tingog sa tambol.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Confuses continuing sound production with an entirely stationary sounding surface.
- B: Confuses oscillation of the membrane with full rotation of the membrane.

**Selection decision:** Specify the continuing sound after the strike; remove stored-sound and rim-escape explanations from the legacy item. Related to guitar vibration, with a different vibrating surface.

**Language evidence:** Primary source and relevant bank question read in English, Tagalog and Cebuano. Shared terminology with earlier batch forms; new sentences remain pending native-language review.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.

**Question provenance:** `slow-vibration-part-vibrating-object-g4` in the newer merged bank. A different card fact ID is an explicit reviewed knowledge link, not an ID substitution.

**Correct option:** B (`o2`).

### English

What vibrates inside a flute to produce its musical sound?

- A: A stretched skin
- B: Air
- C: A plucked string

**Explanation:** Air inside the flute vibrates to produce sound. A vibrating skin or string is used in other kinds of instruments.

### Tagalog

Ano ang nagva-vibrate sa loob ng plawta upang lumikha ng tunog nito?

- A: Nakaunat na balat
- B: Hangin
- C: Pinitik na kuwerdas

**Explanation:** Nagva-vibrate ang hangin sa loob ng plawta upang lumikha ng tunog. Sa ibang uri ng instrumento ginagamit ang nagva-vibrate na balat o kuwerdas.

### Cebuano

Unsa ang nagkurog sulod sa plawta aron makahimo sa tingog niini?

- A: Inat nga panit
- B: Hangin
- C: Gikuhit nga kuwerdas

**Explanation:** Ang hangin sulod sa plawta magkurog aron makahimo og tingog. Ang nagkurog nga panit o kuwerdas gigamit sa laing matang sa instrumento.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Confuses a flute’s air column with the sounding membrane of a drum.
- C: Confuses a wind instrument with the sounding string of a guitar.

**Selection decision:** Add a vibrating-air example rather than another string or membrane rewording. This asks which material vibrates, so equivalence to questions about motion remains unvalidated.

**Language evidence:** Primary source and relevant bank question read in English, Tagalog and Cebuano. Shared terminology with earlier batch forms; new sentences remain pending native-language review.

**Remaining limits and holds:**

- Flute familiarity and the air-column idea need explicit coverage; instrument names alone are not sufficient exposure.
- Teacher, native-language and student-pilot reviews have not occurred.
