## ha-g5-0001 — Recalls matter as having mass and occupying space.

**Revision / origin / status:** 2 / revise / source_checked.

**Target:** `g5-matter-mass-space` · `g5-matter-material-properties` · internal `G5-M-1` (not an official DepEd identifier).

**Curriculum relationship:** describe matter as anything that has mass and takes up space. This item checks only the narrower claim in the heading.

**Source:** `matter-has-mass-takes-space-g4`; cards: `ffct-16553`. Bank item version: 1.

**Question provenance:** `matter-has-mass-takes-space-g4` in the newer merged bank. A different card fact ID is an explicit reviewed knowledge link, not an ID substitution.

**Supporting evidence:** The source explicitly states mass and space occupancy as matter’s defining school-level properties.

**Family:** `matter-mass-space`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

**Use and eligibility:** Incoming grade 6 readiness using grade 5 material candidate; recent-learning candidate only after exposure to this knowledge. Supporting recall/recognition, not evidence of completing a practical activity.

**Correct option:** A (`o1`).

### English

Which pair describes matter?

- A: Has mass and occupies space
- B: Has mass but occupies no space
- C: Occupies space but has no mass

**Explanation:** Matter has mass and occupies space. It need not shine, make sound, or be visible to us.

### Tagalog

Aling pares ang naglalarawan sa matter?

- A: May mass at sumasakop ng espasyo
- B: May mass ngunit walang sinasakop na espasyo
- C: Sumasakop ng espasyo ngunit walang mass

**Explanation:** May mass at sumasakop ng espasyo ang matter. Hindi kailangang kumikinang, tumutunog, o nakikita natin ito.

### Cebuano

Unsang pares ang naghulagway sa matter?

- A: Adunay mass ug nag-okupar og espasyo
- B: Adunay mass apan walay giokupar nga espasyo
- C: Nag-okupar og espasyo apan walay mass

**Explanation:** Ang matter adunay mass ug nag-okupar og espasyo. Dili kinahanglan nga kini mosidlak, motingog, o makita nato.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Retains mass but incorrectly denies that matter occupies space.
- C: Retains occupied space but incorrectly denies that matter has mass.

**Selection decision:** Remove the claim that every quantity can be measured easily and exactly; no measurement is exact without uncertainty. Second pass replaces shine/sound/solubility distractors with parallel statements that each omit one defining property.

**Language evidence:** Primary card and source bank item inspected in all three languages. New wording follows the source meaning and familiar science vocabulary; language approval is still pending.

A wooden block is moved gently from a box to a bowl. What happens to its shape?

- A: It takes the bowl’s shape.

**Explanation:** The wooden block is a solid. Moving it gently to another container does not make it take the container’s shape.

- A: Nagiging hugis-mangkok ito.
- B: Kumakalat ito upang punuin ang mangkok.

- C: Magpabilin ang kaugalingong porma.

**Explanation:** Ang bloke sa kahoy usa ka solido. Dili niini sundon ang porma sa bag-ong sudlanan kon hinay nga ibalhin.

**Selection decision:** Choose a rigid object and gentle transfer, not the false absolute that solids can never deform or melt. Replace the source translation that calls fixed shape broken shape.

**Family:** `liquid-container-shape-volume`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

### English

All the water is moved from a wide cup to a narrow glass. None is lost, and its temperature stays the same. What changes?

- A: Its shape, but not its volume
- B: Its volume, but not its shape
- C: Both its substance and its mass

**Explanation:** The same water takes the shape of the new container. Its volume stays the same if none is lost and the temperature stays the same.

### Tagalog

Inilipat ang lahat ng tubig mula sa malapad na tasa sa makitid na baso. Walang nawala at pareho ang temperatura. Ano ang nagbabago?

- A: Hugis nito, ngunit hindi ang volume
- B: Volume nito, ngunit hindi ang hugis
- C: Ang sangkap nito at ang mass

**Explanation:** Kinukuha ng parehong tubig ang hugis ng bagong lalagyan. Pareho ang volume kung walang nawala at hindi nagbago ang temperatura.

### Cebuano

Gibalhin ang tanang tubig gikan sa lapad nga tasa ngadto sa pig-ot nga baso. Walay nawala ug pareho ang temperatura. Unsa ang mausab?

- A: Ang porma niini, apan dili ang volume
- B: Ang volume niini, apan dili ang porma
- C: Ang substansya niini ug ang mass

**Explanation:** Ang samang tubig mosunod sa porma sa bag-ong sudlanan. Pareho ang volume kon walay nawala ug wala mausab ang temperatura.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Reverses liquid shape and volume behavior.
- C: Ordinary transfer does not change water into a different substance or change the mass when none is lost.

**Selection decision:** State complete transfer; the source-bank residual-water distractor is actually plausible when some liquid stays behind. Temperature constancy is required and supplied in the final wording.
