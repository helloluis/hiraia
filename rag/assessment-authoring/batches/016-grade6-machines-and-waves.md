### English

A source makes one steady tone while moving away from a listener who stands still. Compared with a stationary source, what pitch does the listener hear?

- B: Lower

**Explanation:** The listener receives fewer wave cycles per second, so the pitch is lower. This is the Doppler effect; the source itself can keep making the same tone.
