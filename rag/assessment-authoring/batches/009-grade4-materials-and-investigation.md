## ha-g4-0061 — Recognizes breakdown of material by microorganisms as biodegradation.

**Revision / origin / status:** 1 / revise / source_checked.

**Target:** `g4-biodegradation-by-organisms` · `g4-matter-chemical-properties-of-materials` · internal `G4-M-3` (not an official DepEd identifier).

**Question provenance:** `degradable-vs-biodegradable-g4` in the newer merged bank. A different card fact ID is an explicit reviewed knowledge link, not an ID substitution.

**Family:** `biodegradation-living-organisms`; form equivalence is unvalidated.

### English

Which example shows biodegradation?

- A: Bacteria break down a leaf.
- B: A blade cuts a leaf.
- C: Sunlight dries a leaf.

**Explanation:** Biodegradation is breakdown by living organisms, such as bacteria. Cutting and drying describe different processes.

### Tagalog

Aling halimbawa ang nagpapakita ng biodegradation?

- A: Binubulok ng bacteria ang dahon.
- B: Hinihiwa ng talim ang dahon.
- C: Pinatutuyo ng sikat ng araw ang dahon.

**Explanation:** Ang biodegradation ay pagkasira ng materyal sa tulong ng mga organismo, tulad ng bacteria. Ibang proseso ang paghiwa at pagpapatuyo.

### Cebuano

Unsang pananglitan ang nagpakita sa biodegradation?

- A: Gipadunot sa bacteria ang dahon.
- B: Gihiwa sa sulab ang dahon.
- C: Gipauga sa kahayag sa adlaw ang dahon.

**Explanation:** Ang biodegradation mao ang pagkaguba sa materyal pinaagi sa mga organismo, sama sa bacteria. Lahi nga proseso ang paghiwa ug pagpauga.

**Distractor review:**

- B: Cutting reduces physical piece size without describing biological breakdown.
- C: Drying removes water; that action itself does not describe biological breakdown.

**Selection decision:** Do not treat degradable and biodegradable as mutually exclusive, or chemicals as absent from biological processes. Use three distinct described processes.

**Language evidence:** Source card and candidate bank question read in all three languages. Scientific claims settled before sentence drafting; borrowed science terms retained where useful. Native-language review is pending.

- B: New gas made by the reaction

**Explanation:** The reaction produces carbon dioxide gas, which forms bubbles. Boiling is not needed for this reaction.

### Tagalog

Nagrereact ang baking soda at suka sa karaniwang temperatura ng silid. Ano ang pangunahing laman ng mga bulang nabubuo?

- A: Singaw mula sa pagkulo
- B: Bagong gas mula sa reaksyon
- C: Hangin lamang na nakulong mula sa silid

- C: Hangin lamang nga nasulod gikan sa kwarto

**Explanation:** Ang reaksiyon naghimo og carbon dioxide gas nga mahimong mga bula. Dili kinahanglan ang pagpabukal alang niini nga reaksiyon.

**Distractor review:**

- A: Confuses reaction gas with water vapor from boiling, which the room-temperature context excludes.

**Selection decision:** Specify the reactants and temperature instead of referring to those bubbles without context. Mainly avoids pretending every bubble is perfectly pure gas.
