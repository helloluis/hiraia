# Incoming Grade 3 foundation scope — source review

Reviewed 28 September 2026. This is an author review, not teacher or cohort approval.

The [Anabu II Elementary School learning-resource portal](https://sites.google.com/deped.gov.ph/a2eslrportal/teachers-resources/curriculum-guide-cg), hosted under DepEd's domain, links a readable [August 2023 Makabansa Grades 1–3 guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1Q2IXmfKtG6Chfi6stvpNHdNdNAZJ4W0S/view). The public Drive viewer was inspected directly; no sign-in was used.

The Grade 2 standards on pages 27–31 emphasize community geography, culture, livelihood/services and participation. The Grade 1 scope on pages 15–26 includes personal needs, body care, sound properties, movement, balanced meals and household safety through integrated suggested activities. These are plausible earlier-learning leads, but suggested activities do not establish that every learner was taught twelve particular science facts.

**Decision:** keep incoming Grade 3 prior-grade readiness disabled. Do not relabel Grade 3 science questions as Grade 2 prerequisites. A separate foundation blueprint needs explicit earlier-learning mappings and cohort/teacher confirmation. Current Grade 3 questions can still be considered after verified Hiraia exposure to their tested knowledge; that measures app learning, not prior-grade readiness.

Further review should inspect the applicable early-grade language/health and mathematics guides for individual foundation claims. Do not turn general familiarity or a calendar date into proof of classroom coverage.

## Science guide cross-check

The same school portal links the [full August 2023 Science Grades 3–10 guide](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1C8XgKnMVStLvKiAvhqK8MDQQBxTQjgir/view). Pages 39–42 were cross-checked against the local extract for Grade 5 forces/Earth and Grade 6 materials/living things. Their content matches the selected supporting-knowledge scope. This confirms the extract's source content, not its applicability to every 2026 cohort.

The physical-change/reversibility overgeneralization in Grade 6 materials appears in the guide itself. Assessment drafts use substance identity and stated conditions; they do not teach that every physical change is reversible or every chemical change irreversible. Internal G6 codes remain repository identifiers, not official printed competency codes.
