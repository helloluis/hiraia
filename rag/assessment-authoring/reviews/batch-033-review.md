## Source defects found; production sources unchanged

- `ffct-35154` / `subduction-volcano-arc-g10`, `ffct-32826` / `oceanic-continental-subduction-g8`, and `dcard-08586` / `subduction-recycles-seafloor-g10` present the sinking slab simply melting as the general cause of arc magma. These were excluded as primary sources here. In the ordinary arc mechanism, slab-released water promotes mantle-wedge melting; slab melting can happen in particular settings, so the correction must not assert that slabs never melt. USGS SIM2940's nontechnical pamphlet explicitly supports the water/lowered-melting-temperature explanation.
- `ffct-31206` / `mantle-convection-ridge-upwelling-g10` omits partial melting and says a convection-loop top pushes plates apart. Item 0065 tests only the correctly taught ridge/new-crust location and supplies a magma-cooling explanation. The broader card still needs a source repair before production confidence is claimed.
- The unlinked `asthenosphere-g10` bank explanation says plates sink and melt in that layer. Item 0064 instead uses the current `earth-asthenosphere-plates-ride-g9` card and its bank question.
- `dcard-08627` / `renewable-energy-sources-g10` says renewables cannot be depleted even with continuous use. That blanket claim is false for overused renewable stocks/reservoirs and was excluded.
- `ffct-08603` / `burning-fossil-fuels-carbon-g9` says carbon dioxide was locked underground in fossil fuel; the fuel principally stores carbon in compounds, which becomes CO2 during combustion. This source was excluded in favor of `co2-greenhouse-burning-g8`.
- `ffct-28867` / `el-nino-ph-drought-g9` and `ffct-28868` / `la-nina-ph-rain-g9` overgeneralize national rainfall effects. The assessment uses a stated reduced-rainfall condition and a phase-definition question, respectively.
- `global-warming-definition-g9` offers climate change as a wrong answer even though it is the broader category. Item 0070 removes that overlap and distinguishes long-term global change from a local day or seasonal cycle.
- `thermal-expansion-sea-level-rise-g7` has defective Cebuano phrasing in its bank explanation. Item 0074 is manually rewritten with directly attested expansion/space vocabulary and distinguishes land-ice melt from floating sea ice.
