## Source defects avoided

- `natural-selection-moth-on-bark-g8`: shipping Cebuano stem gives the answer instead of asking a question, and uses a different moth word from its current teaching card. The draft uses the card-attested `anunugba` and a real question. The original unrelated or impossible foils were replaced with artificial selection and individual learning.
- `traits-variation-source-for-evolution-g8`: any differences among organisms are not necessarily genetic. The new stem explicitly says inherited genetic differences.
- `evolution-comparative-anatomy-homologous-g10`: change “proof” to “supports.” Similarity alone is not universally evidence of homology.
- `bread-yeast-co2-rising-g6`: Tagalog confused flour and dough; the draft says `masa`. `nagpapaalsa` was checked against nine current corpus occurrences; the draft's initial `nagpapalsa` typo was corrected before delivery.
- `nata-de-coco-cellulose-bacteria-g9`: Tagalog and Cebuano falsely broaden “not a plant jelly” into “does not come from plants.” Coconut water is the medium. The draft asks the cellulose-producing microorganism and omits that false origin claim. It also avoids testing the obsolete bacterial name as the only valid answer.
- `selective-breeding-vs-genetic-engineering-g10`: do not carry over “same trait much faster” as a guarantee. Direct DNA modification is the assessed distinction.
- `the-need-for-water-g10`: the old generic question treats “increases carrying capacity” as wrong without saying water is scarce. The draft specifies water as the limiting resource and other conditions unchanged, with parallel decrease/same/increase choices.
- `growth-slows-down-g10`: “stops forever” loses “forever” in both translations. The draft uses parallel rate alternatives and states the logistic-model scope, allowing real populations to fluctuate.

Source-bank and teaching-card defects are recorded here only; no production repairs were made.
