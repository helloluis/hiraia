## Findings needing a content or provenance decision

### 1. G10-0022 — the reused wrong option loses “unchanged” in translation

The English wrong option is “Keeps it same”; Tagalog is “Pinapanatili ito” and Cebuano is “Pabilin kini”. The Tagalog wording can describe maintaining blood sugar, which is compatible with insulin's regulatory role, instead of explicitly saying the level does not change. This is a meaning/answer-uniqueness issue, not a register preference.

Suggested replacement for option o3:

- EN: `Leaves the level unchanged`
- TL: `Hindi binabago ang antas nito`
- BIS: `Dili magbag-o sa lebel niini`

The key remains o1. This is currently an exact `reuse`; editing it requires changing the origin to `revise` and refreshing the associated rationale/review record. The ordinary normal-regulation scope is appropriate; this is not a recommendation about insulin treatment.

### 2. G10-0026 — current primary card does not explicitly teach the assessed definition

The question asks for the name of inherited genetic differences among individuals. Its primary card `ffct-12923` / `traits-variation-source-for-evolution-g8` says genetic variation fuels natural selection and beneficial traits are more likely to be inherited. It names the term and its role, but does not directly define the genetic-differences construct being assessed. The bank question does define it; bank text is not proof that a student saw that definition on a card.

The safe choices are to use a separately reviewed definition card, or reframe the assessed target to the role the current card actually teaches. A reframe avoids assuming another card was presented:

- Revised claim: `Recalls genetic variation as the differences on which natural selection can act.`
- EN stem: `Which population feature provides inherited differences on which natural selection can act?`
- TL stem: `Aling katangian ng populasyon ang nagbibigay ng namamanang pagkakaiba na naaapektuhan ng natural selection?`
- BIS stem: `Unsang kinaiya sa populasyon ang naghatag sa mapanunod nga mga kalainan nga mahimong lihokan sa natural selection?`

The existing options and key o2 can remain. These are proposed translations, not native-approved text. Update the target ledger/claim if reframed. Do not automatically add broad “same topic” cards as exposure links.

Candidate-definition retrieval found `dcard-08049` / `genetic-diversity-in-dogs-g8` and `dcard-09193` / `genetic-diversity-defined-g9`, but those explicitly name *genetic diversity*, not *genetic variation*. They should not be silently substituted while retaining the current term question. No replacement link was approved in this review.

## Conditions and explanations to tighten

### 3. G10-0025 — put inherited coloration into the stem

The explanation supplies inherited camouflage, but the scenario itself only describes bark-colored moths surviving/reproducing more. The intended evolutionary-selection example should state inheritance before scoring it. This is a missing condition in the scenario, not a wrong natural-selection key.

Suggested complete stem:

- EN: `Moths with inherited bark-matching color survive and leave more offspring because birds spot them less often. What does this illustrate?`
- TL: `Mas nabubuhay at nagkakaanak ang mga gamu-gamong may namamanang kulay na tugma sa balat ng puno dahil mas mahirap silang makita ng ibon. Ano ang halimbawa nito?`
- BIS: `Mas mabuhi ug manganak ang mga anunugba nga adunay mapanunod nga kolor nga mohaom sa panit sa kahoy kay mas lisod sila makita sa langgam. Unsa kini nga ehemplo?`

The key remains o1. The primary card supports the moth example; the added inheritance is a stated scenario condition. For the role of heritable variation, see [OpenStax Biology 2e, Population Genetics](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/19-2-population-genetics).

### 4. G10-0041 — name the ideal near-Earth, uniform-gravity model

The current statement gives gravity alone/no air resistance, but its constant-horizontal-velocity answer also assumes fixed horizontal/vertical axes and uniform downward gravity. Gravity alone in a general curved/orbital trajectory does not establish the result. The keyed answer is correct in the intended school projectile model; that model should be explicit.

Suggested complete stem:

- EN: `In the ideal near-Earth model, a ball moves under uniform downward gravity with air resistance ignored. What happens to its horizontal velocity?`
- TL: `Sa ideal na modelong malapit sa ibabaw ng Earth, pareho at pababa ang gravity at hindi isinasama ang air resistance. Ano ang nangyayari sa horizontal velocity ng bola?`
- BIS: `Sa ideal nga modelo duol sa nawong sa Yuta, pareho ug paubos ang gravity ug wala giapil ang air resistance. Unsay mahitabo sa horizontal velocity sa bola?`

The key remains o2. G10-0043's 45-degree maximum uses the same ideal near-Earth model; its fixed-speed and equal-height conditions are already present. Add the model qualifier there as well if each question must stand alone. This is a scope clarification, not a reason to change the answer. [OpenStax College Physics 2e, Projectile Motion](https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/3-4-projectile-motion) explicitly uses zero horizontal acceleration and constant downward gravitational acceleration.

### 5. G10-0053 — energy = power × duration needs average or constant power

The kWh key is correct. The explanation's unqualified multiplication can imply that any one instantaneous wattage multiplied by duration gives energy even when appliance power varies. State average power over the same interval, or constant power.

Suggested explanation:

- EN: `A kilowatt-hour measures energy. Average power in kW over an interval, multiplied by that interval in hours, gives energy in kWh.`
- TL: `Energy ang sinusukat ng kilowatt-hour. Ang average power sa kW sa isang interval, pinarami sa tagal nito sa hours, ay nagbibigay ng energy sa kWh.`
- BIS: `Energy ang gisukod sa kilowatt-hour. Ang average power sa kW sulod sa usa ka interval, nga gipadaghan sa kadugayon niini sa hours, naghatag og energy sa kWh.`

The stem, options and key can remain. [OpenStax University Physics, Power](https://openstax.org/books/university-physics-volume-1/pages/7-4-power) distinguishes constant/average power from instantaneous power.

### 6. G10-0045 — make destruction distinct from loss of the kinetic-energy form

Option o2 is English “It is destroyed completely”, Tagalog “Ganap itong nawawala”, Cebuano “Mawala kini sa hingpit”. The latter two can be read as the specified kinetic energy disappearing from that form, which is exactly what the correct conversion answer describes. Spell out that the wrong option claims no conversion into another form.

Suggested o2:

- EN: `It is destroyed without becoming another form of energy`
- TL: `Nawawala ito nang hindi nagiging ibang anyo ng energy`
- BIS: `Mawala kini nga wala mahimong laing porma sa energy`

The key remains o3. This is an ambiguity repair; it does not claim the existing language is ungrammatical.
