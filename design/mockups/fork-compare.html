Fork colour pairs — independent verification

Four proposals, all numbers recomputed from the hexes actually present in each file. Nothing below is a designer’s claim.

The problem

The branch card’s two options are currently painted brick red #B24F35 and teal green #2F6360 — a traffic light. A child reads red as wrong and green as right before reading either word, so a fork that is supposed to offer two equal roads instead nominates an answer.

Each candidate below replaces both fork colours plus the banner/keyline colour (which was also brick, i.e. the same colour as choice A, quietly endorsing it). Gold #D8A03A stays the single-path “next” ticket and teal is demoted to quiz stock in all four.

Requirements under test

  1. Neither colour in the red hue band (HSL <20° or >340°) or the green band (80–170°).
  2. Zero implied valence — no colour that reads as alert, correct, or default.
  3. Cream #F4EAD5 label and letter chip clear WCAG AA (≥4.5:1) on both.
  4. The pair stays separable for dichromats: ΔE00 ≥10 under deuteranopia, protanopia and tritanopia (requirement 8).
  5. Neither collides with the gold “next” ticket (ΔE00 ~15 floor) or with the quiz teal / forest ink.
  6. The banner colour belongs to neither option.
  7. Still mid-century: low chroma, period pigments, no neon or pastel.
  8. Solid colours only — React-Native-portable as backgroundColor.
Method. WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance contrast. Dichromat simulation is Brettel–Viénot–Mollon 1997 two-half-plane projection at severity 1.0 in linear sRGB, using the libDaltonLens precomputed parameters; verified against the textbook cases — pure red renders #A48B00 under deutan and #6C5C0C under protan, pure blue renders #006288 under tritan, all three exact. Distances are CIEDE2000 in CIELAB/D65. Reference point: the incumbent brick/teal pair scores 4.33:1 on its own label (an AA fail) and falls to ΔE00 17.2 under protanopia, where it becomes #6B6036 against #5E5F60 — two muddy mid-tones at the same lightness (ΔL* 0.6). That is the bar every candidate has to beat.

Recommendation — Federal Blue & Dark Ochre (fork-inkpair.html)

Ship it. It is the only candidate that is simultaneously the safest for colour-blind children and the most comfortable on contrast, and it wins those on margin rather than on a tie-break.

  • Colour-blind safety, by a wide margin. Worst case across the three deficiencies is ΔE00 39.0, and the two common ones are 43.4 (deutan) and 42.8 (protan). That is ~4× the requirement-8 floor of 10 and 2.3× the incumbent’s 17.2. No other candidate gets past 33.3. The reason is structural, not lucky: blue-vs-yellow is the one opponent channel protanopia and deuteranopia leave intact, so the simulated blue #2A4C7E is almost identical to the real one.
  • Widest contrast floor. 7.21:1 and 5.09:1 — the highest “weaker member” of the four (coolwarm 4.90, valuesplit 4.82). On a 4.5:1 requirement that headroom is the difference between a pass and a pass you can still tune.
  • Requirement 1 is clean. HSL 216° and 41° — blue and amber, neither in the red band nor the green band, and 41° is 21° clear of the red boundary (twocool’s mulberry is only 9° clear at 331°).
  • Mid-century holds. C* 32 and C* 38 against the brick it replaces at C* 52 and the gold that stays at C* 60 — it is less saturated than the current card. Blue-and-ochre is a literal two-colour schoolbook press job, and the graphite banner is the shared key plate.

Why the others place behind

  • #2 Denim & Tobacco — the same strategy executed slightly thinner. Its numbers were the most honestly reported in the set (everything reproduced exactly), and its banner is the most elegantly neutral thing here: 20.2 from A and 20.1 from B, a bias of 0.1 against inkpair’s 23.7/19.0. But it loses on the three numbers that matter more: contrast floor 4.90 vs 5.09, worst-case CVD 33.3 vs 39.0, and denim sits ΔE00 18.5 from the quiz teal against federal blue’s 23.5 — and under tritanopia denim lands 5.1 from the simulated quiz stock, a genuine (if rare) collision inkpair does not have.
  • #3 Blueprint & Heather — best hue hygiene (225° / 300°, the furthest any candidate gets from both forbidden bands) and the best gold clearance (64.4 / 44.9), but it buys colour-blind robustness with lightness, and lightness is valence. At 18.7 protan it is barely better than the pair it replaces, and the rendered protan and deutan frames show choice B as #5B6375 / #626874 — a true neutral grey, which is how every UI kit draws a disabled control. It also has the tightest contrast in the set (4.82:1) and choice A at L* 22.3 is value-identical to the forest-ink border at L* 22.2, so A’s outline disappears while B’s does not. Two rows that do not look like each other is the opposite of what this brief asks for.
  • #4 Blueprint & Mulberry — the most principled argument (refuse temperature, so neither option can read as the warm alert) and the worst execution of it. Protanopia separation is 16.5, the lowest number in this entire comparison and only 4.9 clear of the brick/teal pair the brief exists to kill; under deuteranopia choice B renders #55575B, a flat grey. It fixes the semantics of the traffic light while barely moving its actual accessibility failure.

If you want to separate #1 from #2

They are close on intent and far apart on only one axis, so decide it on a device, not a spreadsheet. Inkpair’s single real risk is the one its own author names: ochre is gold’s hue family and is held apart from the “next” ticket by lightness alone (ΔE00 27.2, ΔL* 27.9). Put both cards on the Redmi at low brightness in daylight and ask whether choice B reads as “the dark gold one”. If it does, darken the ochre to #6B5114 — I recomputed the author’s fallback and it holds: contrast rises to 6.25:1 and gold clearance to 33.5, so the fix is free. If instead the panel test is clean, ship inkpair as drawn and, if you want coolwarm’s one advantage, lift its perfectly equidistant banner treatment by nudging the graphite a step cooler so it sits equidistant from the two inks rather than 4.7 closer to the ochre.
