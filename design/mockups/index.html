Three visual directions for the factoid card

How to read this. These are three complete, competing looks for the same four screens — a short factoid, a long factoid, a two-way sangandaan fork, and the quiz interject. Pick one direction, or parts of several: the palette, the type, the frame, the button shape, the paper, the fork treatment and the quiz styling are all independently swappable. Everything here is changeable except the cat mascot — the cat is fixed, and each direction is built to sit alongside it. Judge them at a glance first (do they read as different things?), then zoom to 100% and read the small type. Portability notes and a cost verdict live in PORTABILITY.md.
