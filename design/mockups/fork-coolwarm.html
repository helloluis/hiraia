The fork stops being a verdict and becomes a compass: one cool road, one warm road. Red and green are off the board entirely, so nothing on the card can be read as the right answer or the wrong one. Blue-vs-brown is also the one axis that dichromats keep — it rides the blue–yellow channel that protan and deutan leave alone.

Why this reads "different", not "right vs wrong"

A fork should feel like two roads out of one town, and warm/cool is the oldest way a printer says that without ranking anything — a 1950s wall map used slate for water and tan for land, and no child ever thought the land was winning.

Brick-vs-teal failed because red and green are a traffic light: the eye scores them before it reads the words. Denim and tobacco carry no verdict at all, only a change of temperature — and because the banner above them is a neutral graphite that belongs to neither, nothing on the card whispers a preferred answer.

WHAT ELSE MOVED — nothing. Teal #2F6360 stays exactly where it was as the quiz-page stock; it simply stops doing double duty as choice B. The gold ticket, the sage keyline on normal cards, the peach mat and the forest ink are untouched.

What to know before this ships

  • The diff is four lines --brick is deleted. .pick.p-a & .fan-a → #2F4E6B, .pick.p-b & .fan-b → #8A5A32, .band.b-fork + .keyline.k-fork + .forkhead → #4A453D. Card geometry, type, rings, mat and ledges are byte-identical to direction 03.
  • RN-portable by construction Three flat hexes. No gradient, no blend mode, no filter on any swatch. The simulation frames here are literally different backgroundColor values, which is why the cream stock and the engraving look identical across all four — only the fork moved.
  • Honest weak point — tobacco is the tighter contrast 4.90:1 passes AA for normal text and clears AA-large (3:1) with room, but it is the floor of the pair. Do not lighten #8A5A32 toward camel: at L* 47 it drops under 4.5:1 and starts drifting toward the gold ticket. If it ever needs more margin, go darker (#84562F = 5.24:1), never lighter.
  • Honest weak point — tritan pulls denim toward the quiz teal Under tritanopia the denim reads #29515E and the quiz stock #2F6360 reads #35606F — only ΔE00 5.1 apart. They never share a screen (the quiz is its own full-bleed card, gold keyline, four cream options) so it is a non-collision in practice, but if you want it airtight the quiz stock is the thing to move, not the fork.
  • The banner is the quiet fix The old fork banner was brick — the same colour as choice A — so the card was literally shouting one option's name before the child read either word. #4A453D is 20.2 from A and 20.1 from B: a printer's graphite rule that endorses nothing, and still reads clearly apart from the forest-ink band of an ordinary card (ΔE00 17.1).
  • Sits with the cat The mascot is olive tabby, forest outline, gold eyes, peach nose. Denim is the cool this palette never had, and the olive fur pops against it. Tobacco is not a new hue at all: Lab hue 63° against the peach mat's 60° — it is literally the peach of every print mat and the cat's nose taken from L* 76 down to L* 43. So the warm end of the card still belongs to the cat, and the button reads as printed board, not as paint.
