04  Pagsusulit · apat na sagot

React Native port — what is free, what costs

  • The one real cost — knocking out the white The engraving sits on cream, not white, via mix-blend-mode:multiply. RN has no blend modes on <Image>. Three ways out, best first: (1) one-time build step — convert img.png -fuzz 6% -transparent white out.png over the 23,044 assets, then the plain RN Image composites on cream for free; (2) @shopify/react-native-skia <Image blendMode="multiply">; (3) fall back to the white plate (#FFFFFF, in the palette for exactly this) — the design still works, it just reads as a pasted-in label rather than a printed one.
  • Free — the whole card Card, keyline, band, mat, tabs, holes, fans are all View + borderWidth + borderRadius + backgroundColor + absolute positioning. Nothing uses CSS grid, backdrop-filter, gradients or SVG filters.
  • Free — the chunky ledge under every button Deliberately not a box-shadow (Android RN ignores shadow offset and only honours elevation). It is a dark parent View with 4–5px of bottom padding and a slightly larger radius. Ports byte-for-byte.
  • Free — the adaptive type scale The illustration plate is flex:1, minHeight:136; the factoid block is auto. Four steps off string.length alone — no measurement pass: question/answer 18 + 16.5px, ≤120 chars 16.5px, 121–220 15px, >220 14px. Measured windows: 237px (short), 249px (178-char food web), 174px (fork). Stress-tested at the 260-character ceiling on the fork card — the tightest layout in the system — and it still clears the floor.
  • Free — the fanned fork cards Two absolutely-positioned Views with transform:[{rotate:'2.3deg'}]. RN needs an explicit transformOrigin shim (RN 0.74+ supports it; below that, wrap in a translate–rotate–translate triple).
  • Watch — the fat display face Alfa Slab One and Zilla Slab must be bundled as static TTFs and registered per platform. Three families ≈ 6 files ≈ 400KB in the APK. Chivo 900 is the only weight of Chivo used — ship that one file, not the family.
  • Watch — text ellipsis Topic band and choice words use text-overflow:ellipsis; RN equivalent is numberOfLines={1} ellipsizeMode="tail". Long topic labels (“lunar eclipse” is near the limit at 10.5px / 0.15em) truncate rather than wrap.
  • Polish only, safe to drop The 3px drop-ledge under the card and the hairline-plus-diamond divider are decorative; the card is fully legible without them.
