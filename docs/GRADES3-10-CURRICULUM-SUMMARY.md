All 324 competencies in the repository's verified MATATAG baseline are represented across 308 lessons. Added cards and questions include English, Tagalog and Cebuano text. Questions include assessments added to existing facts; they are not all new fact cards. Reachable facts are per grade and can overlap; do not sum the column as a unique library total.

The work mapped categories to competencies, fixed misleading matches, supplied missing practical explanations and quizzes, honored grade-specific quarter order, and preserved access to additional facts through repeat visits. Thin pools finish before their target rather than repeating padding. Curriculum representation does not certify mastery or replace hands-on assessment. The entire source library has not been individually re-reviewed.

Validation: 67 tests and the mobile type-check passed. Grades 3–8 reach simulations remain unchanged by the final Grades 9–10 integration. These changes have not yet been built into or deployed as an APK.
