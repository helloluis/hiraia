# Full-year Revised K-12 science curriculum audit

5 October 2026. Reviewed the archived DepEd three-term Science Budgets of Work for **Grades 3–10, all three terms**, extending the Term 2 weeks 4–9 pilot. Every one of the **322 listed competencies** has explicit reviewed teaching and quiz selections across **1,032 authored slots** and **1,280 distinct selected cards**, including approved related examples. This is instructional coverage, not proof of practical performance or mastery.

The extension adds 257 competencies / 827 slots to the previous 65 / 205. It adds **42 teaching cards**, **35 question additions/replacements on existing facts**, and **20 persistent factual corrections**. The website competency catalogue was regenerated from the same lesson manifests.

## What was repaired

- **Grade 3:** Length-unit slots selected animal-size trivia; oil disposal selected oil-fire physics; sky observation selected moon counts/orbits; light-message slot selected invention dates/fiber technology. Explicit teaching/quiz selections; new safe sorting, ruler measurement, message testing and sky-record activities; six focused question replacements/additions.
- **Grade 4:** Life-cycle names/sounds substituted for stages; high-altitude trivia substituted for circulation; soil slots lacked comparable procedures; weather recording lacked numerical interpretation; unsafe lightning crouch instructions. Explicit selections, five trilingual activities, five questions on existing cards, and cross-bank lightning safety corrections.
- **Grade 5:** Broad mass/temperature slots admitted animal trivia; life-system slots favored incidental facts; mineral slot selected gemstones rather than soil formation; electrical practicals lacked complete controlled procedures. Explicit selections; five new activities/reference cards; six question additions/replacements; PAGASA wind ranges verified 2026-10-05. Preserve the plant/animal adaptation term split.
- **Grade 6:** Separation benefits, moving receivers, local volcano risk, and attributed sky knowledge needed more direct instruction; Balatik was incorrectly assigned to Ursa Major. Explicit selections retain sound existing practicals; four new trilingual cards, four focused questions, and a sourced card/quiz/grounding correction for Balatik.
- **Grade 7:** Regex matches admitted measurement trivia and missed explicit practical steps, local fault causes, and heat-transfer tradeoffs. Some cell and graph statements required limits. Five new trilingual teaching cards, two equipment questions, and explicit selection for every remaining official slot. Retain accurate existing practicals and their limits on litmus, microscope observations, solubility, solvent volume and graph direction.
- **Grade 8:** Missing explicit family-generation modelling, particle comparison, storm plotting and local motion observation. Wrong or ambiguous statements about coaster crests, negative work, fixed composition, domains and PAGASA. Five trilingual practical/example cards, five focused question overrides, four source corrections, and reviewed selections across the remaining official slots.
- **Grade 9:** Definitions were standing in for controlled motion tests, circuit tradeoffs and collaborative map work; oversimplified bond-change and reliability claims could mislead investigations. Five trilingual teaching cards, five focused questions, two persistent science corrections, and explicit selections for all remaining slots. Include the Term 2 weeks 10–11 ecosystem diagrams and survey block previously outside pilot scope.
- **Grade 10:** Missing controlled momentum/rate tests, multiple indicators, full electricity route and group activities. Momentum and impulse cards omitted conditions or called inertia a force. Nine trilingual practical/example cards, two focused questions, four source corrections and reviewed selections for every remaining official slot, including Term 2 weeks 1–2 and 10–11.

Persistent fixes cover eight unsafe lightning cards plus a grounding-only variant; Balatik/Orion; coaster energy at the crest; negative work; fixed composition ratios; the molecular basis of three-domain classification; physical versus chemical changes; repeatability versus validity; and momentum, inelastic collisions, stopping force and inertia. Nineteen rendered cards and nineteen grounding facts are affected, with overlapping sets. [Exact correction list and language handoff](FULL-YEAR-CEBUANO-HANDOFF.md).

The final metadata check also replaced the overbroad “Bond Changes Are Chemical” title, qualified four retrieval topics, and removed three mismatched/overgeneralized illustrations on momentum, collisions and seatbelts. The corrected source overlays run after generation. Lesson compilation accepts explicit reviewed IDs and excludes unapproved reserve matches. Smaller pools are deliberate: most listed lessons now contain 3–11 relevant cards rather than padding toward the old 20/30-card maximum. Optional-pool planner tests use a dedicated larger fixture, while real lessons are checked for complete anchors, distinct facts, saved-run behavior and feed traversal. Narrowing an empty JSON reserve also exposed and fixed a `never[]` TypeScript inference in the lesson type.

## Evidence and limits

- Original PDFs, extracted text, editions, URLs and checksums: `rag/sources/curriculum-guides/three-term-2026/`. The inventory verifies every PDF SHA-256. Grade 9 has mixed printed footer dates; the archived edition evidence is preserved.
- BOW headings/ranges are authoritative; `G3-M-1` style codes and teaching slots are Hiraia identifiers, not official DepEd codes. Source text is preserved even when an example needs scientific qualification (e.g. sugar dissolving is not a reaction-rate experiment).
- Reviewed decisions: `rag/pipeline/full-year-review.json` plus `term2-pilot-review.json`. The English content lock ties reviewed IDs to actual teaching and effective question text; translation-only changes can preserve the lock.
- This pass reviews the selected Calendar teaching path and fixes confirmed defects found during that review. It does not certify the full discovery library, all illustrations, native fluency, school assessment results, or hours of instruction.
- Teachers still assess actual measurement, graphs/diagrams, practical controls, group discussion, research and local preparedness plans. Offline explanatory cards do not supply every external source, live alert or local evacuation plan.

Scientific references attached to decisions/corrections include [NWS lightning guidance](https://www.weather.gov/safety/lightning-crouch), [National Museum constellations](https://www.nationalmuseum.gov.ph/exhibitions/planetarium/), [PAGASA wind signals](https://pagasa.dost.gov.ph/learning-tools/tropical-cyclone-wind-signal), [PHIVOLCS volcano alerts](https://www.phivolcs.dost.gov.ph/volcano-alert-levels/), [OpenStax domains](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/22-introduction), [EDF Rance tidal plant](https://www.edf.fr/sites/groupe/files/2024-12/rance-tidal-power-plant-security.pdf), [NHGRI mutations](https://www.genome.gov/genetics-glossary/Mutation) and [NHTSA airbags](https://www.nhtsa.gov/vehicle-safety/air-bags). Soil water retention was checked against [USDA NRCS](https://www.nrcs.usda.gov/sites/default/files/2022-10/nrcs142p2_051590.pdf). These are educational references, not claims of current local warning conditions.
