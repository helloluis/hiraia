# Hiraia licensing terms — proposed version 1.0

Prepared 28 September 2026. **DRAFT — NOT AN OPERATIVE LICENSE GRANT.**

This document is proposed wording for approval and scope mapping. It does not replace any
existing license. The legal licensor and approved coverage manifest are not yet confirmed.
The software section is custom legal wording, not a lawyer-reviewed standard license.

## 1. Purpose and scope

Hiraia is intended to remain freely usable for noncommercial learning, teaching, and
community deployments. Commercial permissions for eligible Hiraia-owned material are a
separate decision of its rights holder.

“Licensor” means the legal person identified in the approved coverage manifest.
“Covered Material” means only the specifically identified files or artifact versions, and
the rights in them, that the manifest explicitly places under these terms. Each entry must
identify its license, licensor, version or hash, and exclusions. A repository location,
download link, or Hiraia brand name alone does not make something Covered Material.

These terms grant only rights the Licensor controls. Third-party material, earlier license
grants, and statutory rights remain unaffected. Where a file contains both Hiraia additions
and existing material, the manifest must identify the additions and retain the earlier
notices. An unclear boundary is not permission to impose new restrictions on the whole file.

## 2. Educational content

Original educational text, quizzes, explanatory diagrams, and documentation explicitly
designated as Covered Content are licensed under **Creative Commons
Attribution–NonCommercial 4.0 International (CC BY-NC 4.0)**.

The unmodified [CC BY-NC 4.0 legal code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/legalcode.en)
governs that grant. The descriptions here are explanatory and do not add restrictions to it.
Recipients may copy, share, translate, and adapt Covered Content for noncommercial purposes
subject to the license. When sharing, retain required attribution and license information
and indicate changes. Do not imply that Hiraia endorses an adaptation.

Suggested attribution, with the manifest's actual licensor and artifact reference filled in:

> Hiraia — © [rights holder], [year]. [Artifact/version and source URL].
> Licensed under CC BY-NC 4.0. Changes: [description, if modified].

Attribution can be provided in a reasonable manner under the license; this suggested layout
is not an additional mandatory format. Existing third-party credits must remain intact.

CC BY-NC is not ShareAlike and does not require publication of every private adaptation.
It also does not grant exclusive rights in facts or otherwise unprotected material.

## 3. Software

### Previously licensed code

Code already distributed under Apache 2.0, MIT, or another license remains available under
those grants, including any commercial rights they provide. This document does not cancel
them. The historical Apache contribution notice must be accounted for when determining
eligible new scope. Changing a filename, packaging code in an APK, or adding a notice does
not extinguish prior permissions.

## 4. Models, datasets, voices, and other dependencies

Each model, adapter, voice, dataset, font, sound, image, and library retains its identified
upstream license and attribution requirements. A Hiraia commercial permission cannot waive
another rights holder's conditions.

In particular, Meta MMS-derived voice models retain their upstream noncommercial restriction
unless the relevant upstream rights holder grants an appropriate separate permission.
Do not describe a commercial agreement for Hiraia's own work as permission to commercialize
the complete current APK with all bundled assets.

No blanket new license is applied to Hiraia-trained weights or raw training corpora by this
document. Their release notices must identify exact artifacts, upstream checkpoints,
modifications, relevant source constraints, and rights in Hiraia's additions separately.
Model-license restrictions and rights in generated outputs are distinct questions.

Previously CC BY-licensed illustrations, CC0 sounds, Apache/MIT code, and independently
licensed fonts remain subject to those grants. Photographs and source documents without
confirmed relicensing rights are excluded from the new content grant.

## 5. Branding

No general trademark license to the Hiraia or Tala names, logos, or glyphs is granted here.
Accurate attribution and lawful descriptive references remain allowed. Do not represent a
modified app, hosted service, or commercial product as an official Hiraia release without
authorization. This reservation does not reclaim upstream font rights or earlier grants in
artwork, and does not assert that any particular mark is registered.

## 6. Commercial permissions

Commercial permission is **opt-in and written**, not inferred from silence, a donation,
repository access, an APK download, or the applicant's institutional status. This applies
only where a commercial license to the Licensor's rights is actually needed.

An agreement should specify:

- The legal parties and the Licensor's authority to grant the identified rights.
- Exact material, versions/hashes, covered rights, and third-party exclusions.
- Authorized product/service, deployment, redistribution, modification, sublicensing, term,
  and territory, as appropriate.
- Fees or an explicit fee waiver, and any agreed requirements for free student access.
- Attribution, trademark permissions if any, and support responsibilities if any.
- Confirmation that upstream noncommercial material has been separately cleared or replaced.

There is no promise to grant commercial permission and no standard fee implied here.
The Licensor may deliberately grant a free commercial permission for a specified project.

## 7. Contributions and ownership

Existing contributor permissions remain valid. Before accepting future contributions for
which the project needs a separate commercial-licensing option, record the contributor's
identity/authority, material, ownership, third-party inclusions, and an explicit written
grant permitting that commercial relicensing. A contribution under an NC license alone is
not automatically permission for the project to sell a commercial license to it.

AI-generated material must not be described as exclusively owned merely because Hiraia paid
for generation or review. Preserve provider terms and source provenance, and assert rights
only to the extent they exist and the Licensor controls them.

## 8. Publication and future changes

Publish the completed coverage manifest and applicable notices alongside each release to
which this policy applies. Future versions may carry different terms, but a later policy
change cannot withdraw valid earlier grants from compliant recipients.

Before activation, replace this draft status with the effective date, confirmed legal
licensor and commercial contact, and approved manifest reference. Do not replace existing
Apache/CC BY/CC0 notices with this document or add NC headers to unresolved existing files.
