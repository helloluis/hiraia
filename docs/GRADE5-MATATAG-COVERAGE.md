# Grade 5 MATATAG coverage crosswalk

Audit date: 2026-09-09. Reference: the August 2023 MATATAG Science Grades 3–10 guide extracted in `rag/sources/curriculum-guides/matatag-elementary-competencies.json`. This is the curriculum version the app currently implements; it is not a verification of later curriculum revisions.

## Finding

**Broad subject coverage: yes. Fully verified competency coverage: not established by labels alone.** All 40 Grade 5 competencies have existing candidate cards. The 43 grade-prefixed subcategory IDs have 42 distinct English names because waste management appears in both MATTER and EARTH_SPACE. Several subcategories are supplementary, and several required objectives are bundled into one subcategory.

This is a manual semantic crosswalk of every Grade 5 subcategory and every Grade 5 competency, plus an inventory check and targeted text inspection. It is not an exhaustive correctness or instructional-completeness audit of every card. Competency codes below are Hiraia’s internal identifiers for the guide’s numbered objectives.

The subcategory card counts below count only explicit membership in that grade-prefixed label. They are NOT the full grade-appropriate pool: the app also contains many valid generic labels. The competency counts include those cards through the existing competency tags. Cards can belong to several competencies; counts are not additive.

## Verified examples and issues

- Life-cycle content is present: `ffct-39010` covers young mammals; `ffct-39011` and `ffct-39012` cover birds maturing and reproducing; `ffct-39014` covers carabao development; `ffct-39015` and `ffct-39020` cover plant development. There is no dedicated Grade 5 life-cycle subcategory among these 43 IDs, so explicitly allocate these cards when assembling the L-6 lesson.
- Rock-classification content is present: `ffct-37528`, `ffct-37529`, `ffct-37533` and subsequent cards explain observing features and using a rock key. However, `ffct-11575` and `ffct-14543` are about organism identification and are also tagged E-3. They may teach a transferable method but cannot substitute for the rock-specific objective.
- Volume measurement has 12 competency-tagged cards, below either 20- or 30-card run target. Examples `ffct-15333`, `ffct-18173`, `ffct-18392` cover a graduated cylinder and meniscus reading. Check the remaining cards for mL/L and beaker coverage; do not pad the run merely to hit 20.
- Water-cycle model content exists (`ffct-34454`, bag experiment), as does circuit diagram content (`ffct-23529`, `ffct-25248`) and electromagnet construction (`ffct-22208`). Their presence does not verify that a learner can actually construct or investigate: reserve appropriate observation/practice tasks in a complete instructional plan.
- The Q4 weather objective contains time-sensitive warning-signal material. This audit checks topic alignment, not current PAGASA thresholds; verify those separately before treating every warning-signal card as authoritative.

## Sequencing consequences

1. Use the guide’s quarter and competency order as the required first-run checklist.
2. Map generic and grade-prefixed labels into each lesson; do not discard already-labeled generic cards or repeat the labeling pass wholesale.
3. Split broad groups into objective coverage slots (e.g., rock observation, rock classification, soil formation; life cycles of mammals, birds, plants). These slots need not require another exhaustive taxonomy.
4. Keep enrichment available for later visits rather than counting it as mandatory Grade 5 coverage.
5. Assign 20/30-card runs only after selecting cards that cover each lesson’s distinct objectives. A nonzero tag count or a completed run is not proof of competency mastery.
