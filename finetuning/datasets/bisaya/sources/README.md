### Access Barriers Encountered

1. **SDO Cebu Province Portal** (lrms.sdocebuprovince.com)
   - Lists the Bisaya-titled modules in their catalog
   - No direct download links available without authentication
   - URL patterns tested: 404 errors for all attempted download paths
   - Requires login credentials (likely DepEd employee account)

2. **DepEd Cebu City Portal** (depedcebucity.com)
   - Explicitly states: "Only with DepEd Account can access the different links"
   - Restricted to DepEd employees only

3. **National DepEd LRMDS Portal** (lrmds.deped.gov.ph)
   - Has related Grade 3 Science modules
   - Most modules are in English, not Bisaya
   - Found related resources in Kinaray-a (different Visayan language)
   - Requires authentication for downloads

4. **DepEd Tambayan** (depedtambayan.net)
   - Successfully downloaded modules from here
   - These are English versions from Region XI (Davao), not Bisaya versions
   - Direct PDF links work without authentication

### Module Content Structure

The downloaded modules follow the DepEd Alternative Delivery Mode format:

**Module 1: Classifying Objects and Materials**
- Lesson 1: Objects and Materials Around Us and their Properties
- Lesson 2: Solid Objects/Materials and their Characteristics
- Lesson 3: Liquid Objects/Materials and their Characteristics
- Lesson 4: Gaseous Objects/Materials and their Characteristics

**Module 2: Changes in Materials**
- Lesson 1: Changes from Solid to Liquid (melting)
- Lesson 2: Changes from Liquid to Solid (freezing)
- Lesson 3: Changes from Liquid to Gas (evaporation)
- Lesson 4: Changes from Solid to Gas (sublimation)

### Bisaya Text Found

While the downloaded modules are in English, the SDO Cebu portal listing confirms these Bisaya terms are used:

- **Mga Kinaiya** = Characteristics/Properties
- **Liquids ug Gases** = Liquids and Gases
- **Mga Kausaban** = Changes
- **Materyales** = Materials
- **base sa Temperatura** = based on Temperature
- **base sa Epekto sa Temperatura** = based on Temperature Effects

## Conclusion

While the SDO Cebu Province portal confirms the existence of Grade 3 Science modules with Bisaya titles (Mga Kinaiya sa Liquids ug Gases, Mga Kausaban sa Materyales, etc.), the actual PDF files are not publicly accessible. The modules appear to be restricted to authenticated DepEd personnel. I was able to download the English versions of equivalent content from Region XI, which cover the same topics (classifying materials and changes in materials) but in English rather than Bisaya.
