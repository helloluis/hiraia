Ulo, Abaga

Ulo, Abaga
Posted at 02:26h in Songs, Videos by Danilo-Ramon Guillano 0 Comments

Use this children’s song to teach different parts of the body in Cebuano.

Lyrics

Ulo, abaga, hawak, dapidapi
Tuhod, tiil, tuhod, tiil

English Translation

Head, shoulder, waist, butt cheeks
Knee, feet, knee, feet
