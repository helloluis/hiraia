Magngalan Ta

Magngalan Ta
Posted at 04:59h in Songs, Videos by Danilo-Ramon Guillano 0 Comments

Teachers can use this animal children’s song for identifying animals in Bisaya language.

Lyrics and English Translation

Magngalan ta karon (Let’s identify now)
Pangalan sa mga hayop (Name of animals)
Ako si iring (I’m a cat)
Ako sab si iro (I’m a dog)
Ako si baka (I’m a cow)
Ako sab is kabaw (I’m a water buffalo)
Ako si kanding (I’m a goat)
Ako si kabayo (I’m a horse)
