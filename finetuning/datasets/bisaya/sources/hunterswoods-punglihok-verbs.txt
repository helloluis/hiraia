MTB Sinugboanong Binisaya
Mga Punglihok o Berbo (MTB Sinugboanong Binisaya Worksheets)
A very quick introduction to verbs -- called mga punglihok in Sinugboanong Binisaya -- and free worksheets to help with mastery.

Verbs are called mga punglihok in Sinugboanong Binisaya. The word punglihok is a combination of pung (a contraction of pulong, which means word) and lihok (which means action). Thus, punglihok literally means action word…or verb!

Meaning of punglihok in Bisaya / Cebuano / Sinugboanong Binisaya:

Mga pulong nga nagpahayag ug aksyon

Punglihok in English is:

Verb

Punglihok in Tagalog is:

Pandiwa
