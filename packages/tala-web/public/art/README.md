# Spot illustrations for the teacher portal

Decorative drawings for a few quiet places in the app (the Library's opening, empty states, the "make these slides
yours" invitation). They are **not** science content and are not part of any teaching pack; pack drawings come only
from Hiraia's reviewed card art and keep their Hiraia mark.

Made on 19 Nov 2026 with an OpenAI image model (`gen-image`, quality high), then converted to greyscale WebP with the
paper pushed to pure white so the ink sits on the cream page (`mix-blend-mode: multiply`, class `.ink`).

Shared style line: "Vintage copperplate engraving illustration with fine cross-hatching and stippling, like a plate
from a nineteenth-century science textbook. Pure black ink on a pure white background. No grey wash, no colour, no
border, no frame, no text, no letters, no numbers, no labels. Generous white space around the subject."
