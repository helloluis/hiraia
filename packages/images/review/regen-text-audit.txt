REGEN BAKED-IN-TEXT CALIBRATION AUDIT
adjudicated 2026-08-21 | read-only pass | companion file: regen-text-audit.csv

VERDICT: the calibration gap is REAL and large. The zero-flag batches (003/006/007/010)
under-detected baked-in text; their "0 of 150" is a detection failure, not a quality
difference. Recommend a targeted re-review before the library merge.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ROOT CAUSE (this is the important finding)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
506 of the 2,612 regeneration prompts (19.4%) explicitly instruct the image model to draw
a word, letter or number - e.g. "A calendar page showing the number 365", "the element
sulfur with the letter S and the number 16", "The word MEGA with the number 1,000,000".

That is by design: build-regen-worklist.py routes 'text' verdicts down the KEEP-SUBJECT
path, reusing the ORIGINAL prompt, on the stated premise that "the boilerplate fix
addresses those failure classes". It does not. For text-as-subject prompts the model
simply complies. Roughly one regeneration in five was structurally incapable of fixing
the defect it was queued for.

Explicit-text prompt density per batch (this predicts where the defects are):
  003 50.5% | 006 37.5% | 011 32.8% | 010 28.6% | 009 22.9% | 002 19.8% | 012 15.1%
  008 14.1% | 005 13.5% | 001 10.9% | 004  9.4% | 007  7.8% | 013  0.9% | 000  0.0%

The range is dominated by the 7-of-64 base-rate sample (95% CI ~4.5%-21%). The lower bound

Medium on the exact total for the full 2,612 - the extrapolation to the un-sampled batches
leans on prompt-density scaling, not on direct observation.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. RECOMMENDATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DO NOT merge the library on the current verdicts.

1. Fix the pipeline first, or round 3 reproduces this exactly. In build-regen-worklist.py,
   send 'text' verdicts to the REWRITE path, not keep-subject: the subject itself has to
   be restated without the word/letter/number (e.g. "a calendar page with a blank date
   cell" instead of "showing the number 365"). Regenerating a text-as-subject prompt with
   nicer boilerplate cannot work.
2. Mandatory re-review, tier 1: the 506 images whose prompt explicitly requests text
   (~32 sheets, one pass). Hit rate there is ~47%, so ~200 of the ~205 estimated defects
   sit in this 19% of the library. This is the cheap, high-yield sweep.
3. Tier 2: sample ~15% of the remaining 2,106 to bound the residual; escalate to full
   re-review only if that sample lands above ~3%.
4. Re-review all 14 batches against ONE written bar, not just the four zero-flag ones -
   batch 011 detected 36.5% on the same prompt class where this audit found 47%, so even
   the strictest pass is leaving roughly a quarter of its own class on the floor.
5. Set the bar as "legible glyphs inside the artwork". Decorative garble should be a
   separate, lower-severity verdict: 2 of the 3 garble claims checked here did not survive
   magnification, and the harness caption strip must never count.
